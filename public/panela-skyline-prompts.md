# Illustrated chapter transitions

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, with transparent backgrounds. PNG sources are preserved beside the optimized WebP assets.

- `panela-skyline-green`: An ultra-wide whimsical vintage game city silhouette with crooked rooftops, chimneys, water towers, distant trees and antennae. Single deep moss green (#253a22) screenprinted shape with subtle dry ink texture, irregular roof contour, transparent sky, continuous opaque bottom. No text, characters, gradients or realistic shading.
- `panela-skyline-gold`: An ultra-wide vintage cartoon industrial city silhouette with an arched bridge, quirky brick towers, water tanks, chimneys, curling smoke and cables. Single muted ochre brown (#443720) screenprinted shape, transparent sky above the roofs and continuous opaque bottom. No text, characters, gradients or realistic shading.

The page uses the alpha channels as masks so each skyline joins its section background without a color seam. The existing rooftop illustration was restored only in the hero.
